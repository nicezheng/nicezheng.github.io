\section{Research Interests}
My research connects social computing, human--AI decision-making, and AI for
science. I study how people and AI use social signals, how large language
models represent human values, and how domain and physical knowledge can make
scientific AI more useful and reliable.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Human--AI Decision-Making in Social Systems:} social signals,
    unverifiable decisions, and the behavioral consequences of making hidden
    activities visible to people and AI.
  \item \textbf{LLM Values and AI Orientalism:} cultural value
    representations, representational asymmetries, and links between model
    behavior and training-data provenance.
  \item \textbf{AI for Geospatial Science:} automated and interactive dune
    segmentation, expert correction, tangible interaction, and
    cloud--edge--device collaboration.
  \item \textbf{AI for Ocean Science:} physics-informed spatiotemporal ocean
    prediction and early warning of marine heat extremes.
\end{itemize}
