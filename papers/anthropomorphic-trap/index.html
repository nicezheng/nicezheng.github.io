--- layout: paper paper_id: anthropomorphic-trap title: 'Escaping the Anthropomorphic Trap: Resolving the Cognitive Mismatch in Future AI Software Development' description: For more than a half-century, the foundational paradigms of software development—such as modularity, information hiding, and hierarchical labor division—have been engineered to accommodate the strict cognitive limitations of human working memory. However, the rapid emergence of multi-agent artificial intelligence exposes a fundamental cognitive mismatch. Current frameworks attempt to integrate high-dimensional, stateless neural reasoning engines by coercing them to operate within these legacy, human-centric structures, falling into an anthropomorphic trap. This friction generates a novel, highly volatile behavioral entropy defined as AI-software technical debt, wherein amnesic agents hallucinate opaque workarounds to bypass architectural blindfolds. To resolve this, we propose Invisible Software Engineering (Invisible SE), a paradigm shift from agent-centric human emulation to environment-centric stigmergic governance. By reimagining the repository as an active computational topology which utilizes topological gradients, temporally weighted memory, and representation-space regularization, Invisible SE safely guides AI behavior without classical code-level constraints, thus transcending the cognitive horizons of human developers. This environment-centric protocol offers a potential blueprint for orchestrating scalable, long-lived computational infrastructures that transcend the cognitive horizons of human developers and enable autonomous agents to work, coordinate, and scale to build future software systems. permalink: "/papers/anthropomorphic-trap/" page_class: paper noindex: false ---
